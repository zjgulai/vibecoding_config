# ADR format

Use this only for an authorized, ADR-worthy decision. Follow an established local convention when it exists.

```markdown
# ADR: <decision title>

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
- Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Scope: <affected context>

## Context
The durable problem, constraints and evidence that made a decision necessary.

## Decision
The chosen direction and its boundary.

## Alternatives and trade-offs
| Alternative | Benefits | Costs / reason not chosen |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Consequences
Expected gains, accepted costs, migration or compatibility implications, and signals that would justify reconsideration.
```

Do not mark a proposal as accepted without the authority that owns the decision.
